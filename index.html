Legal Analysis

Jan 28, 2026

Executive Summary

This bill mandates transparency requirements for federal agencies deploying AI systems in decision-making processes affecting the public. It establishes an AI Transparency Office within the OMB, requires algorithmic impact assessments for high-risk systems, and creates a public registry of AI tools used in government services.

Statutory Framework

Amends the E-Government Act of 2002 (44 U.S.C. §3501 et seq.) to add a new subchapter on artificial intelligence transparency in federal information management.

Regulatory Scope

Applies to all executive branch agencies as defined in 5 U.S.C. §105, with phased implementation beginning with CFO Act agencies.

Key Provisions

Algorithmic Impact Assessments
§3(a)

Requires agencies to conduct and publish impact assessments before deploying AI systems that affect individual rights, benefits, or access to government services.

Compliance:

Annual certification by agency CIO

Enforcement:

GAO audit authority, congressional reporting

Public AI Registry
§5

Establishes a searchable public database of all AI systems deployed by federal agencies, including purpose, data sources, and performance metrics.
